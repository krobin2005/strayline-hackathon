# North Hall College | Account recovery
Practice runbook. North Hall College, its systems, and the incident are fictional.

## Unexpected account recovery
When someone reports an account-recovery request they did not initiate, leave pending requests on hold. Open an identity-review ticket with the campus help desk. Verify the account owner through the campus directory's existing callback route.

## Recording the report
Record the event time, the recovery status, and the observer's description. Preserve the original notification or screen recording. Record which details are reported by the caller and which appear in the system. An unexpected request alone does not establish that an account was compromised.

## Routing tickets
Assign unrequested recovery events to Identity Operations. The on-call analyst reviews repeat events first. Record each distinct ticket once when counting repeat reports.
